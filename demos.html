06 / BEFORE WE START

Questions worth asking.

What if the assistant doesn’t know an answer?

We configure it to use your approved business information and direct unknown or out-of-scope questions to an agreed human contact. AI can make mistakes, so this behaviour is part of testing and ongoing review.

Can a person take over?

A route to your team is agreed during setup. This may be a notification, contact request or a supported inbox handoff. The exact experience depends on the channel and tools you use.

Which accounts and subscriptions do I need?

The current demonstration uses Botpress, Google Calendar and Airtable, with WhatsApp for the conversation. Your setup may use a website channel instead. We identify the necessary accounts, account ownership, access and third-party charges in the proposal.

What does ongoing support include?

If you choose ongoing support, the proposal defines monitoring, troubleshooting, information updates, response times and revision limits. New workflows and integrations are quoted separately.

What happens if I stop the service?

We agree ownership, handover, exports and cancellation terms before starting. Accounts you own remain yours; any functions relying on cancelled hosting or subscriptions may stop. Continued operation depends on keeping the required services active.

Will this bring me new leads?

This service handles enquiries you already receive. It does not include advertising or promise more website traffic, sales or a particular conversion rate.

How is customer information handled?

We agree which information is needed, where it is stored and who can access it before launch. Your customer-facing privacy information should cover the tools you choose. Please do not submit sensitive information in the demo chat.
